## Relationships between Finnic & the rest of the troupe
Finnic and Ingamar both joined the troupe as children, so they've been close ever since then.
Emory joined the troupe not long after, so the three of them have mainly stuck together.
Thrornala, Arel, and Elora are the longest tenured members of the troupe. Most likely they have been with Umidden since the beginning, but Finnic has never been able to get an answer as to how long ago that was.
The two human clowns are completely different people once the show is finished, which Finnic is not a big fan of. He hopes that more of the members were more genuine and true to their nature when they were performing.
Pim always acts like a clown, even when the lights have turned off, so Finnic and him get along just fine.
The two strongmen tend to occupy their time either training or fighting each-other, which Finnic respects, but he tends to keep his distance from them.

Umidden has a habit of adding members to the troupe as they travel around, people he finds interesting, people he thinks could be entertaining, lost souls. He's also quite an absent minded individual, so the troupe often-times finds themselves stuck at gates or borders while he scrambles to get the proper paperwork filled out. People leaving the troupe, people joining the troupe, and random pop-up shows often happen when they are in this kind of situation.
