The north is made up of nearly fifty independent city states with the old capital at the center of a lake in the north. Most are ruled by powerful dark elf houses, while three tiefling cities mountain passes and rich mineral deposits to the west. Slightly north of the Wall where the campaign starts, a handful of southern aligned city states act in interest of the south kingdom and in return they receive grain and trade and military aid, creating tension with the rest of the north. All of them trace their roots back to the old empire that ruled the region until the southern kingdom shattered it six hundred years after the Hellmarch. Since then, no one has been able to reunite the north, though more than a few still dream of restoring the empire under their own banner.
